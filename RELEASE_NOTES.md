# MAL+ v0.7.0 (Beta)

First public beta release of MAL+ for Android.

### Highlights
- Modern .NET 11 and .NET MAUI architecture with fluid gesture navigation and anchored navigation bar.
- Complete MyAnimeList library tracking (Anime & Manga) with customizable card status indicators and badges.
- **Exclusive Discovery Hub (Experimental / WIP):** "Because You Liked..." recommendation engine, Season OP/ED themes, Random Gems, and "Adapted to Anime" manga filter.
- Integrated AnimeThemes streaming player powered by AndroidX Media3 (ExoPlayer) for creditless openings and endings.
- Native community forums, seasonal rankings, and personalized broadcast airing calendar.
- Highly customizable poster cards (format chip, score display mode, release season chip).
- Automatic background update checks and manual changelog viewer in Settings.
- 100% private: local on-device diagnostics, zero tracking or third-party telemetry.
